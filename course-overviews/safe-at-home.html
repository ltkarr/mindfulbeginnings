course overview

Safe@Home

Virtual class. Students join a live Zoom class from home with a device and a camera. There are no class materials to bring and no hands-on kit.

Students learn how to take care of themselves when they are home without an adult, how to avoid the situations that go wrong, and exactly what to do when something does.

A NOTE ON READINESS

Finishing this class does not mean a child is ready to stay home alone. That call belongs to the parent, and it depends on age, maturity, experience and confidence. What the class does is give a child the safety training piece, plus the language to talk with you about the rest.

Maryland does set an age. A child under eight may not be left alone in a home or a car. Most families find the year or two after that is the right window for this class, when independence is new and the habits are still being formed.

Students who are ready for more, or who will be watching a younger sibling, can move on to our Safe Sitter® Babysitting course in grades six through nine.
