Every Mindful Beginnings instructor completes this onboarding once. It walks you through the instructor portal, covers the essentials from the Instructor Guide, and ends with a short quiz. Plan on about 25 minutes. Your quiz result is saved to your instructor record when you pass.

Lesson 1. Getting into the instructor portal

The portal is where everything happens: open jobs, your confirmed jobs, the full Instructor Guide, and your account documents.

  1. Go to instructorportal.mindfulbeginnings.org.
  2. Sign in with your PIN. Lindsay gives you your PIN when your paperwork is complete. Keep it private; it is how the portal knows the jobs on your screen are yours.
  3. Forgot your PIN? Text Lindsay at 703-987-5979.
  4. If the portal says your account is not active, your onboarding paperwork is not finished yet. Nothing is broken and nothing was deleted; contact Lindsay to finish it.

The five tabs

  • Open jobs: classes you are eligible to teach that still need an instructor.
  • My jobs: the classes you have accepted, with everything you need for class day.
  • Instructor Guide: the complete guide, plus teaching materials and resources. The lessons below are the highlights; the guide is the reference.
  • My account: your profile, credentials, and document uploads.
  • Partnerships: only liaisons see this tab.

Lesson 2. Finding and accepting a job

  • You only see jobs you are approved to teach. Your approved course list reflects the credentials and training on file. If you believe you are eligible for another course, contact Lindsay.
  • Each open job card shows the course, the date and time, the location including the street address, and the group size, so you can judge distance and fit before you commit.
  • Jobs flagged “En español” must be taught completely in Spanish. They only appear for instructors marked as Spanish-speaking.
  • When you are sure you can teach a class, tap “Accept this job.” Accepting is a commitment: the class comes off the board and the host counts on you.
  • After you accept, the host’s cell number and WiFi unlock on your job card in My jobs. For virtual classes, the Zoom link is sent to you after you accept.
  • Not sure about a job? Ask Lindsay before you accept it, not after.
The one rule of the board: if it is in your My jobs, it is yours. Put it on your calendar the moment you accept.

Lesson 3. Before every class

Preparation

  • Preview your materials several days ahead. Never open the course materials for the first time in front of students.
  • Your roster is emailed one day before class. If it shows no students registered, registrations are probably handled by the organization and we cannot see them; we will send what the organization gives us or at least the headcount.
  • Arrive 15 minutes early. Set up the room and test the technology before students walk in.
  • Fill out participant certificates at the end of the session, never before.
  • Pace the lessons so every required module is fully covered before the scheduled end time.

Picking up materials

  • Materials are picked up from the lockbox before class and returned to the storage box after. The address and the code live in the Instructor Guide inside your portal, so they stay behind your PIN.
  • Pick up no more than three days before your class, unless you arrange an earlier pickup with Lindsay.
  • Check the kit immediately. Cross-reference everything against your teaching checklist as soon as you pick up, and check every compartment of every bag before you report anything missing. If something really is missing, tell Lindsay before your class date, not the morning of.
  • Your Safe Sitter® Instructor Manual is yours to keep after onboarding. Bring it to every Safe Sitter® class you teach.

Lesson 4. Teaching essentials

Safe Sitter® babysitting courses

  • Teach the Introduction module first. After that, teach the remaining modules in whatever order works best for your group.
  • Teach the CPR module before Life and Business Skills whenever you can, while students still have the energy for hands-on practice.
  • If you finish everything early and evaluations are done, show the Babysitter Boss videos.
  • Build in breaks, and pair the lunch break with a lecture-heavy module such as Child Care Skills. The host provides a meal or students bring their own; instructors bring their own meal.
  • Mindful Beginnings provides small bingo prizes (you can also give them to volunteers). If you run a raffle instead, we supply the tickets and the grand prize is your responsibility.

Safe@Home

  • Have students complete the Safe@Home student evaluation before the class ends. This applies to every Safe@Home session, in person and virtual.

Hands-on CPR and choking practice

  • Manikins only. Students practice compressions, rescue breaths, back blows, and abdominal thrusts on the manikins. They never practice on each other, and you never demonstrate on a student.
  • The child manikin is a torso, not a full body. It goes on the floor and students kneel beside it. Clear enough floor space for the whole group to kneel, and tell students in advance so they dress for it.
  • Every student uses an individual face shield.
  • Stay on your feet and circulate during practice so you can correct positioning and stop horseplay before it starts.

Lesson 5. Equipment and tech, quick fixes

  • Before class: install fresh lung bags in the manikins and check batteries. Screwdrivers are in the kit for battery covers.
  • No indicator lights? Remove and reinstall the batteries. That fixes most power issues.
  • Chest does not rise? The lung bag is probably seated wrong. Reseat it. If it still fails, skip rescue breaths and have students do compressions while saying aloud that they are delivering breaths.
  • Safe Sitter® presentations need an internet connection and your instructor account (through YUDU). Mindful Beginnings modules open by logging into your email on the company laptop.
  • Test everything before students arrive. If the WiFi fails, verify your credentials and restart the laptop. If casting to a TV does not work, use the USB-C to HDMI cable in the kit.
  • Total tech failure: teach the course cleanly without slides. Stay calm, use your instructor guide, and keep the class moving.

Lesson 6. Virtual classes on Zoom

  • You need an active Zoom account; a free personal account is fine.
  • Use the meeting link Lindsay sends before the session.
  • Join a few minutes early and claim host right away: Participants, then Claim Host, then the host key (it is in the Instructor Guide in your portal). Host controls let you manage the waiting room, mute, and share your screen.
  • Open the Google Slides link from Lindsay and share your screen immediately.
  • Test your audio and video first, keep your guide open or printed nearby, and keep your background clean, quiet, and professional.

Lesson 7. When plans change

If you need to cancel a job

  • More than one week out: cancel through the portal.
  • Fewer than 48 hours out: finding a replacement is your responsibility. Contact the instructor sub list right away.
  • Emergency: if you have worked the sub list and cannot find coverage, call Lindsay immediately at 703-987-5979.

If the client or Mindful Beginnings cancels

  • Clients, schools, and partners reschedule and cancel, sometimes the day before or the morning of. Flexibility is a core expectation of this role.
  • If a class is rescheduled, we make every effort to offer you the new date before the job is reposted. That is standard practice, not a guarantee.
  • Cancelled in advance: unpaid. There is no pay for preparation time, travel, or calendar time held for a class that does not take place.
  • Cancelled on the class day: paid in full, whether or not you had already arrived.
  • Return the materials to the storage box within 48 hours either way.

Lesson 8. After class, pay, and expectations

Returning materials

  • Return everything to the locked storage box within 48 hours of the end of the course (for a multi-week series, after your final class), neatly and in original condition, including extra supplies.
  • Exception: if you are teaching the same class again within the next week, keep the kit for that class instead of returning it in between.
  • Make sure the storage box is locked before you leave, and text Lindsay at 703-987-5979 once the materials are back, so she knows they have been returned.

Pay

  • Pay is issued when the course or series is complete, all materials are returned, and participant evaluations have been submitted.
  • Rate increases are merit-based, based on courses taught and evaluation feedback, and are always communicated in writing.

Professional expectations

  • Wear your Mindful Beginnings ID badge at every in-person session.
  • Refer a private, in-home course that is held with 6 or more participants and you earn a $50 bonus. Referring an organization earns you first priority to teach that course (no cash bonus).
  • Questions about anything in this onboarding: Lindsay, 703-987-5979 or lindsay@mindfulbeginnings.org.

After School Clubs

  • Clubs meet one hour a week, right after dismissal, and are taught by certified teachers and registered nurses. We bring everything; the school provides the classroom, dismissal to you, and a pickup plan.
  • My First Babysitter’s Club is an 8-week series only (8 to 12 kids). The other clubs run 8 or 10 weeks with 8 to 16 kids, and a club needs a minimum of 6 students to run.
  • Every club is also offered in Spanish.
