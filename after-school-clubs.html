after school clubs

We come to your school and bring everything. Ten clubs for elementary schools, taught by certified teachers and registered nurses.

Bring clubs to your school

At a glance

Grades K to 5 · One hour a week, after dismissal · 8-week series · 8-16 kids (minimum 6) · Taught by certified teachers and registered nurses

We bring everything: instructor, materials, and take-home pages. Every club is also offered in Spanish.

Title I schools, ask about discounted pricing. Club pricing is per participant, set with your school.

The clubs

My First Babysitter's Club

The first step toward babysitting: a trusted extra set of hands while a parent is home, with real practice on dolls, props and role play.

Grades 4 to 5

Little Medics

A first aid club for the youngest helpers: what an emergency is, how to get help fast, and how to handle scrapes, bumps, bug bites and nosebleeds.

Grades K to 2

Pet Sitter's Club

For kids who want to care for animals and earn a little money doing it: what pets need, staying safe around animals, and running a real pet sitting job.

Grades 3 to 5

Snack Squad

A kitchen club with no stove required: a new snack every week, plus kitchen safety, clean hands, food allergies and reading labels.

Grades 2 to 5

Kid Boss Club

Young entrepreneurs turn an idea into a real product or service, then pitch and sell it at a pop up on the final day.

Grades 4 to 5

Middle School, Here I Come

A head start on the biggest school change yet: opening a locker, reading a schedule, keeping track of six teachers and speaking up.

Grade 5

Big Sibling Club

For kids with a baby or little sibling at home, or one on the way: gentle, safe ways to help, and reassurance that big feelings are normal.

Grades K to 3

My First Phone

For kids getting a first phone, or about to: passwords, privacy, group chats, screen time, and what to do the moment something online feels wrong.

Grades 4 to 5

Walk and Roll Ready

Getting around the neighborhood safely on foot, bike or scooter: rules of the road, what to do if they get separated, and planning their real route.

Grades 2 to 5

Calm and Ready

A feelings toolbox for every kid: name what they feel, notice it in their bodies, and use simple tools that work in the moment.

Grades K to 5

Bring clubs to your school

Schools choose the clubs and the days: as a standalone offering, through an after school enrichment provider, or as a PTA program.
