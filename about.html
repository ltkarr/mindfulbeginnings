about

Mindful Beginnings teaches safety and caregiving skills to kids, teens, parents, grandparents and workplaces across the DC metro area. Every class is taught by a registered nurse or certified educator, and we bring everything to you.

Founder & President meet Lindsay!

Lindsay Karr has spent more than two decades doing what she does best: teaching. She holds a B.S. in English Education from Boston University, a Master's in Educational Psychology from the University of Virginia, and post-graduate coursework in Administration and Supervision from Johns Hopkins University.

For over 20 years, she has been working in education. She taught in public school classrooms, where she took pride in building meaningful relationships with her students. Today she supervises college seniors through their student teaching year, mentoring the next generation of educators as they take charge of their first classrooms.

She founded Mindful Beginnings on a simple conviction: safety is a teachable skill, and every child, and every adult who cares for one, deserves to learn it from someone who truly knows how to teach.
