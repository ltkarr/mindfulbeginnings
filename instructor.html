Pay policy
Pay for each job is listed as your hourly rate times a set number of hours. That total usually includes at least 1.5 hours beyond the class time, and we may add more depending on the job's location. Pay hours are set at Mindful Beginnings' discretion. Please do not ask for additional pay for any course. If the listed pay doesn't work for you, simply don't claim the job.
