Badge workshops at your meeting.

Half-day workshops, full-day intensives, and troop meeting visits for First Aid, Babysitting, and Safety badges. We bring the training to your meeting location. Custom workshops can match your troop's age group, goals, and schedule. Pricing varies by workshop and group size.
