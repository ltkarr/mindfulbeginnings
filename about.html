Founder & President

Lindsay Karr is the founder and president of Mindful Beginnings, which provides essential safety training and caregiving education taught by registered nurses and certified educators. Programs teach critical skills including home-alone safety, responsible care, basic first aid, rescue techniques, and CPR. In addition to instructor-led courses, Lindsay’s team develops custom training materials and educational programming for organizations.

Lindsay holds a degree in English Education from Boston University and a Master’s in Educational Psychology from the University of Virginia, and she has completed post-graduate coursework in school administration at Johns Hopkins University. With more than 20 years of experience teaching students of all ages, she currently supervises student teachers in the DC area.

The idea for Mindful Beginnings emerged when Lindsay searched for a high-quality babysitting course for her oldest daughter. Local classes lacked flexibility and convenience, so she created a solution that brings training directly to students. She is passionate about helping people of all ages stay safe while learning to care for others responsibly.
