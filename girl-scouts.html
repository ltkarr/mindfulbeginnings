Girl Scouts

Girl Scout badge workshops

Looking to help your troop earn their First Aid, Babysitting or Safety badges? We have got you covered. Mindful Beginnings offers half-day workshops, full-day intensives and troop meeting visits, where we bring the training directly to your meeting location. We also design custom workshops tailored to your troop's age group, goals and schedule. Pricing varies based on workshop details and group size. Contact us for a custom quote.

Daisy (grades K–1 / ages 5–7)

  • Good Neighbor: Community safety helpers (firefighters, EMTs, nurses) and how to ask for help.
  • Safety Award Pin: Basic street, home and fire safety rules.

Brownie (grades 2–3 / ages 7–9)

  • First Aid Badge: Treating minor injuries (burns, stings, nosebleeds), making a mini first aid kit and practicing emergency responses.
  • My Best Self Badge: Health, wellness, stress management, body awareness and self-care habits.
  • Safety Award Pin: Preventing home accidents, simple fire safety plans and safe behavior around pets and strangers.
Request a troop workshop

Junior (grades 4–5 / ages 9–11)

  • First Aid Badge: Handling choking emergencies, treating sprains, burns and poison exposure, and calling emergency services under pressure.
  • Staying Fit Badge: Mental health, stress relief techniques, balanced nutrition and daily movement routines.
  • Safety Award Pin: Internet safety basics, neighborhood safety and home alone rules.
Request a troop workshop

Cadette (grades 6–8 / ages 11–14)

  • Babysitter Badge: The quintessential caregiving badge, covering child development stages, entertaining kids, infant and toddler safety, diapering and feeding basics, emergency planning and running a babysitting business.
  • First Aid Badge: Tending to serious injuries (shock, fractures, head injuries), triage basics and CPR awareness.
  • Science of Happiness Badge: Stress management, mindfulness strategies, emotional regulation and psychological well-being.
  • Eating for You Badge: Nutrition for energy.

Senior (grades 9–10 / ages 14–16)

  • First Aid Badge: Hands-on CPR/AED training, treating severe wounds, hypothermia and heatstroke, and handling wilderness or sports injuries.
  • Women's Health Badge: Physical and emotional health, managing stress and self-advocacy in healthcare.
Request a troop workshop

Ambassador (grades 11–12 / ages 16–18)

  • First Aid Badge: Advanced emergency response, disaster readiness and teaching first aid skills to younger troops.
  • Coaching Badge: Mentoring younger kids in sports and safety, and leadership in caregiving.
