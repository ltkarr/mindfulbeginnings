A NOTE ON THIS COURSE

All Kids Welcome is part of our Advanced Babysitting Series and builds directly on the skills students learned in their first babysitting course. This is not a medical course. Students do not learn to diagnose, assess or treat any condition. They learn to show up prepared, communicate well with parents, adapt their care to fit each child, and stay calm when things are hard.

Families of children with special needs often have a hard time finding babysitters who are ready and willing. Graduates of this course leave as more confident, more valuable sitters who can make a real difference for those families.
