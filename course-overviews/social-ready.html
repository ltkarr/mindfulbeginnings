Social Ready: Safety Skills for Life Online

A workshop for grades 4 to 8, in person or live on Zoom

Most kids get a phone long before anyone teaches them how to use it safely. Social Ready is a 60 minute workshop that gives kids the skills before they need them: what to post, what to protect, who to trust, how to handle a group chat that turns mean, and what to do the moment something feels off.

This is not a lecture and it is not a scare talk. Every student moves, votes, sorts, and practices throughout the hour. Nobody just watches.

WHAT FAMILIES TAKE HOME

Every student leaves with the Social Ready Family Tech Agreement, a sheet to fill out together at home. It covers what the student promises, what the parent promises, where phones sleep at night, and the three trusted adults the student will go to first.

"If you come to me with a problem, I will help first." That line belongs to the parent. Kids hide online problems because they are afraid of losing the phone. This agreement takes that fear off the table.

WHAT THIS COURSE IS AND IS NOT

Please note: Social Ready is an education and skills workshop. It does not recommend any app, and it does not tell families when a child should have a phone or social media. That decision belongs to each family. The class gives kids the skills and gives families a place to start the conversation.
