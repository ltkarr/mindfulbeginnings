course overview

Steady and Ready™

Staying safe and confident, my way. A neurodivergent affirming safety class for kids and teens who are building independence at home, taught at a pace that fits how they learn.

AT A GLANCE

AGESAges 8–14
LENGTHSixty minutes, with a built in brain break
FORMATIn person or virtual
GROUP SIZE8–12 participants
TAUGHT BYRegistered nurses and certified educators

WHO THIS CLASS IS FOR

Kids and teens ages 8–14 who are neurodivergent, including autistic learners and learners with ADHD, anxiety, sensory differences or learning differences

Learners who found a standard safety class too fast, too loud, or built on skills they were still developing

Families who want a calm, affirming first step toward independence at home

HOW THE CLASS FEELS

A visual schedule at the start, so learners always know what comes next

One idea at a time, with real pauses for processing

Fidgets, headphones, comfort items and a quiet spot are all welcome

Breaks anytime, no questions asked

Literal, respectful language, with no idioms or sarcasm

Effort is celebrated, and listening counts as taking part

GETTING CALM AND STEADY

Square breathing, practiced together

Building a personal calm down toolkit

Noticing when the body feels too big or too fast

Choosing a strategy that works, from counting to a quiet spot

STAYING SAFE AT HOME

Doors, windows and locks

What to do when someone comes to the door

Kitchen and heat safety

Care around water and stairs

WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

When to call 911, and why it is always okay to call

Exactly what to say, including name, address and what happened

A plan for the phone ringing and for an unexpected visitor

What to do when a situation feels unclear or unsafe

Low pressure practice cards, out loud or thought through quietly

MY SAFETY PLAN

Two trusted adults, with a name and phone number for each

Home address, and what to tell 911

The tools and the quiet spot that help this learner feel calm

A clear first step for any surprise: breathe, then check the plan

WHAT IS INCLUDED

A registered nurse or certified educator as your instructor

A short family intake before class, so we know what helps your child

A printed agenda and a My Safety Plan worksheet for every learner

A certificate of completion for every learner

A NOTE FOR FAMILIES

Steady and Ready teaches staying calm, staying safe at home, knowing what to do when something goes wrong, and building a personal safety plan.
