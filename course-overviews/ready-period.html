Ready. Period.™

A Puberty Prep Workshop for Girls

Grades 5, 6 & 7 • Girls • One 60 minute workshop • 6 to 18 participants

Workshop Overview

Family life class taught girls how it works. This workshop tells them what it is actually like. Girls get honest answers from a registered nurse, handle every product with their own hands, and ask anything through an anonymous question box. Every girl leaves with a stocked mini period pouch small enough to keep in a backpack or a locker.

Her mini period pouch Every girl goes home with a mini period pouch, already stocked with pads in two absorbencies, liners, tampons, a pocket tracker card and an encouragement card. It is sized to live in a backpack, a locker or a gym bag, not a bathroom drawer at home, so that when the day comes she is not searching for a supply closet. At home, families add wipes and spare underwear in a sealed bag, and restock it the week she uses something.

The Sunset on Midsummer edition When Ready. Period. is hosted at Sunset on Midsummer at the Shops at Wildwood in Bethesda, the 60 minute workshop is followed by a 30 minute private shopping event. The store is closed to the public for our group, so girls browse with their friends, and the shop's on site personalization studio can add her name or initials to whatever she picks out. The whole evening runs 90 minutes.

A Note on This Workshop

Ready. Period. is a workshop, not a class. There are no slides, no posters and no lectures. The nurse sits with the girls rather than standing in front of them, and every question is answered honestly in a room where nothing is too embarrassing to ask.

Our nurse shares what is typical and when something is worth a conversation with a parent and doctor. She does not diagnose, examine or give individual medical advice, and no girl is ever asked about her own development. Girls leave prepared, confident and ready for whatever comes next.
