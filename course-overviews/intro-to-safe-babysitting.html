A NOTE ON THIS COURSE

Intro to Safe Babysitting is a first step, not a full babysitter training. Students learn about child development, daily care routines and behavior management. The course does not cover first aid, choking rescue, CPR or babysitting business skills, and it does not issue a Safe Sitter® certificate. Completing this program does not mean a student is ready to babysit. That decision belongs to each family, based on the maturity, skills, experience and confidence of their child.

Students who are ready for more can continue with our full Safe Sitter® Babysitting course, which adds hands on training in choking rescue, first aid, introductory CPR skills and running a babysitting business, along with a Safe Sitter, Inc. certificate.
