We come to you

Mindful Beginnings is based in Bethesda, Maryland, and brings hands-on safety and caregiving workshops to your location anywhere within 60 miles of Washington, DC. Founder Lindsay Karr, M.Ed., is an educator with more than 20 years of experience teaching students of all ages. She started Mindful Beginnings in 2026 after searching for a flexible, high-quality babysitting course for her oldest daughter and not finding one. Classes are taught by registered nurses and certified educators.
