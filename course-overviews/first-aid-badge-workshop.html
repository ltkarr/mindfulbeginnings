First Aid Badge Workshop

FIRST AID BADGE WORKSHOP COURSE OVERVIEW Real supplies. A staged injury. Scouts treat it start to finish. Designed for Girl Scout troops in Girl Scouts of the Nation’s Capital (GSCNC)

Hands-on first aid for Girl Scouts, taught by a registered nurse. This workshop puts real supplies in scouts’ hands and walks a staged injury from Check through Care.

AT A GLANCE

WHO IT IS FORGirl Scout Brownies through Ambassadors (First Aid badge at each level)
LENGTH60-minute core workshop (default) · Optional 120-minute full scenario circuit
GROUP SIZEWhole troop; multi-level troops and service unit events welcome
TAUGHT BYA registered nurse (second instructor for larger groups at no extra charge)
REGIONMaryland, DC and Virginia; designed for GSCNC troops

AFTER COURSE, SCOUTS WILL BE ABLE TO

Explain and use Check–Call–Care as their first aid decision frame

Decide when to call 911, practice a clear call script and assign the call to a specific person

Identify core first aid kit contents and pack or build one at their level

Put on gloves and treat common minor injuries (scrapes, nosebleeds, burns, bee stings, basic sprains) with real supplies

Apply one outdoor or urgent skill matched to their badge level

Walk a staged injury from scene check through bandaging at the scenario station

WHAT IS INCLUDED

Travel to your meeting space across Maryland, DC and Virginia

All supplies and first aid materials for hands-on practice

Student handouts for every scout

Projector and laptop if the site needs them

A written badge-step completion summary for the troop leader

You provide the room, a wall or screen, your scouts and the adult ratios your council requires.

HOW BADGE CREDIT WORKS

Mindful Beginnings delivers the instruction and hands-on practice. The troop leader confirms which badge steps were completed and purchases badges from the Girl Scout Shop (or through the troop’s usual process). Mindful Beginnings does not issue Girl Scout badges and does not claim official GSUSA endorsement. This workshop is designed around the official Girl Scout First Aid badge steps for GSCNC troops.

If a host asks Mindful Beginnings to provide the physical badges, we can arrange that as an add-on.

SCOPE AND DISCLAIMER This is an educational, hands-on practice workshop. It is not a formal CPR or First Aid certification course. It is not affiliated with the American Red Cross, the American Heart Association or any other certification body, and it does not issue certification cards. Skills taught here support Girl Scout First Aid badge work and everyday readiness. They do not replace professional medical care, EMS response or a certified CPR/First Aid course.
