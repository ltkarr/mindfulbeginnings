Choose your path

Private home

You are hosting friends, neighbors or family at your home. Tell us about your space and the course you would like, and we will confirm the date. Prices are per participant, and as the host, your own registration is free.

Host at my home

Organization

You are hosting for a school, troop, congregation or other group. The organization form collects the day-of contact, room details, billing and any multi-day schedule. Your organization can cover the full cost, cover a portion, or have participants register and pay individually on a private Mindful Beginnings link.

Host for my organization

Who counts as an organization?

Any group hosting outside a private home, including:

  • Schools and PTAs
  • Houses of worship and congregations
  • Girl Scout troops (see our Girl Scouts page for badge workshops)
  • Homeschool groups and co-ops
  • Workplaces and small businesses (see group CPR certification)
  • Camps, sports teams, community and volunteer groups

Hosting a group of friends at your house? Use the private home form instead.

For hosts: Home hosts receive one free registration (this applies to private home classes only). We bring all the materials, equipment, supplies and the instructor. Questions before you book? Call 703-987-5979 or email lindsay@mindfulbeginnings.org.
