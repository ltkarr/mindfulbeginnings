Girl Scouts

Girl Scout badge workshops

Looking to help your troop earn their First Aid, Babysitting or Safety badges? We have you covered. Mindful Beginnings offers half-day workshops, full-day intensives and troop meeting visits, where we bring the training directly to your meeting location. We also design custom workshops tailored to your troop's age group, goals and schedule. Pricing varies based on workshop details and group size. Contact us for a custom quote.
