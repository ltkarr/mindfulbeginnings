Season Ready™

Safety Skills, Fueling, and Injury Prevention for Student Athletes

Most of what limits a young athlete is not talent. It is an underfueled body, a lack of recovery, and a culture that treats reporting pain as weakness. Season Ready is a 60 minute, hands-on workshop taught by a Registered Nurse to help middle and high school athletes stay healthy, perform at their peak, and handle critical emergencies.

Our RNs bring all materials directly to your team, athletic department, club, or parent hosted group. Every student practices hands-on. Nobody just watches.

WHAT ATHLETES LEARN AND PRACTICE

Performance and Recovery Smart pre and post training fueling with no calorie counting or weigh-ins, hydration tracking, science backed warmups, and why sleep is actual training.
Injury Awareness How to tell the difference between normal muscle soreness and injury pain that needs to be reported.
Emergency Response Hands-on practice with hands-only CPR manikins, AED trainers, rapid cooling for heat stroke, and real world concussion scenarios.
