1. Courses you are approved to teach

Your approved course list reflects the credentials and training on file with Mindful Beginnings. If you believe you are eligible for an additional certification, contact Lindsay.

2. Before class
Preparation

Review materials early. Preview the course and all digital resources several days ahead. Never open materials for the first time in front of students. Your roster is emailed one day before class. If you see that no students have registered, it is likely because registrations are handled through the organization, and we cannot see it on our side. We will do our best to send the roster to you from the organization or at minimum tell you how many students are registered for the session.

Arrive 15 minutes early. Set up the room and test the technology before students walk in. Do not fill out any participant certificates until the end of the session.

Watch the clock. Pace the lessons so every required module is fully covered before the course is scheduled to end.

Picking up materials

Location. Lockbox at 8803 Bradmoor Drive, Bethesda, MD 20817. Code: 2026.

Timing. Pick up no more than three days before the class begins, unless you arrange an earlier pickup with Lindsay.

Check immediately. Cross-reference everything against your teaching checklist as soon as you pick up. Report anything missing or unclear to Lindsay before your course date, not the morning of class. Check every single compartment of every single bag before you report something is missing.

Safe Sitter® manuals. You keep a permanent hard copy of the Safe Sitter® Babysitting Course Instructor Manual after onboarding. Bring it to every Safe Sitter® class you teach.

Other courses. Facilitator guides are provided in hard copy at pickup or sent electronically. Access everything in the Instructor Resources section below.

3. Teaching the Safe Sitter® Babysitting Course
Module order and pacing

Introduction first. After the Introduction module, teach the remaining modules in whatever sequence works best for your group.

CPR early. Teach the CPR module before Life and Business Skills whenever possible, so students still have the energy and time for hands-on practice.

Sessions over 5 hours. Choose any additional modules to fill the remaining time.

Finishing early. If all required modules and evaluations are complete, show the Babysitter Boss videos.

Breaks and lunch. Build in standard breaks and pair the lunch break with a lecture-heavy module such as Child Care Skills. The host provides a meal or students bring their own. Instructors are responsible for their own meal.

Prizes

Standard setup. Mindful Beginnings provides small bingo prizes. You also may use these prizes to give to volunteers.

Raffle alternative. Hand out raffle tickets to bingo winners and active volunteers during class, then draw for one larger prize at the end. Mindful Beginnings supplies the tickets. The grand prize is your responsibility.

Videos

These are the video files that can be saved in the event that Wi-Fi is not strong. They can be played in a media player. You can save them to the laptop. See the full download list in Instructor Resources below.

4. Hands-on CPR and choking practice

Manikins only. Students practice compressions, rescue breaths, back blows, and abdominal thrusts on the manikins. They never practice on each other, and you never demonstrate on a student.

Plan for the floor. The child manikin is a torso, not a full body. It goes on the floor and students kneel beside it to practice. Clear enough floor space for the whole group to kneel comfortably, and tell students in advance so they dress for it.

Choking rescue with the torso manikin. The Child Choking Rescue video shows a full-body child manikin that the rescuer kneels behind. Our child manikins are torsos, so kneeling behind them is not possible. Do your best: kneel behind the torso and simulate the practice as shown in the video.

Face shields. Every student uses an individual face shield.

Supervise closely. Stay on your feet and circulate during practice so you can correct positioning and stop horseplay before it starts.

5. Equipment and technology
Manikins

Before class. Install fresh lung bags. Install batteries if needed. Screwdrivers are provided to help with battery installation.

No indicator lights. Remove and reinstall the batteries. This resolves most power issues.

Chest does not rise. The lung bag is most likely seated incorrectly. Reseat it, or omit rescue breaths and have students perform compressions while stating aloud that they are delivering breaths.

AV and WiFi

Who handles what. Mindful Beginnings arranges whether AV is needed at a site, so that is not your concern. Knowing how the on-site equipment works before class begins is.

Accessing presentations. Safe Sitter® presentations require an active internet connection and your instructor account. Access Safe Sitter® digital resources through YUDU. All MB modules are shared with you and open by logging into your email on the company laptop.

Test first. Log in and open your presentation before students arrive. If the WiFi fails, verify your credentials, restart the laptop, and try again.

Chromebook display. Casting to the host television is easiest when the television is on their network: open the presentation, click the three dots at the top of the screen, select Cast, and choose the device. If casting is unavailable, connect to the projector with the USB-C to HDMI adapter cable. If the screen does not display correctly, click the clock in the bottom corner, go to Device, and adjust the mirror display setting.

Total tech failure. Teach the course cleanly without slides. Rely on your instructor guide, stay calm, and keep the class moving.

7. Schedule changes and cancellations
If you need to cancel a job

More than one week out. Cancel through the portal. Waitlisted instructors are notified automatically.

Fewer than 48 hours out. Securing a replacement is your responsibility. Contact the instructor sub list right away.

Emergency escalation. If you have exhausted the sub list and cannot find coverage, call Lindsay immediately at 703-987-5979.

If the client, host organization, or Mindful Beginnings cancels

Expect change. Clients, schools, and partners reschedule, cancel, or fall below minimum enrollment, sometimes the day before or the morning of class. Flexibility around scheduling is a core expectation of this role.

Rescheduled dates. We make every effort to offer you the new date before the job is reposted to the portal. This is our standard practice, not a guarantee.

Cancelled in advance: unpaid. Mindful Beginnings does not pay for preparation time, travel, or calendar time held for a course that does not take place.

Cancelled on the class day: paid in full. You receive your full scheduled pay whether or not you have already arrived at the site.

Materials. Return them to the storage box within 48 hours.

8. After class
Returning materials

Drop-off. Return all materials to the locked storage box at 8803 Bradmoor Drive, Bethesda, MD 20817 within 48 hours of course completion. For a multi-week series, return materials after your final class.

Condition. Return everything, including extra supplies, neatly and in original condition.

Security. Confirm the storage box is locked before you leave.

Pay

Timing. Payment is issued at the completion of a course or series, once all materials have been returned and participant evaluations have been submitted.

Cancelled courses. A course that does not take place is unpaid, with one exception: a course cancelled on the scheduled class day is paid in full.

9. Professional expectations

ID badges. Wear your official Mindful Beginnings ID badge while teaching and bring it to every in-person session.

Private course referrals. Earn a $50 bonus when a private, in-home course you referred is successfully held with a minimum of 6 participants.

Organizational referrals. Referring an external organization gives you first priority to teach that course. Cash bonuses do not apply to organizational referrals.

Pay increases. Hourly rate increases are merit-based and at the sole discretion of Mindful Beginnings LLC, based on the number of courses taught and feedback from participant and parent evaluations. All increases are communicated in writing.
