Babysitting

From first jobs to pro-level skills.

Safe Sitter® Babysitting with CPR Module

Ages 11–14 • 5 hours • In-person • 6–16 participants

Youth gain the skills and confidence to begin babysitting safely: CPR, choking rescue, first aid, infant care, and child development.

Safe Sitter® Babysitting Essentials

Ages 11–14 • 4 hours • In-person • 6–16 participants

Tweens learn safety, child care basics, and behavior management for beginning babysitters.

Red Cross Babysitter’s Training + Pediatric First Aid/CPR/AED

Ages 11–14 • 7 hours • In-person • 6–12 participants

Teens learn child care, safety, and leadership skills, practice infant and child CPR on manikins, and earn American Red Cross Babysitter’s Training and Pediatric First Aid/CPR/AED certifications.

Babysitter’s Training

Ages 11–15 • 4.25 hours • In-person • 6–12 participants

Teens learn child care, safety, and leadership skills for beginning babysitters, with hands-on practice.

Introduction to Safe Babysitting by Safe Sitter®

Ages 10–12 • 60 minutes • In-person or virtual • 6–20 participants

A 60-minute intro for participants curious about babysitting.

All Kids Welcome™: For Experienced Sitters

Experienced sitters • 60 minutes • In-person or virtual • 6–20 participants

An advanced course for sitters who want to confidently and inclusively care for participants with diverse needs.

Kids Safety

Real-world readiness for real-world moments.

Safe@Home: First Steps to Independence

Ages 8–10 • 90 minutes • In-person • 6–20 participants

Designed to prepare upper-elementary participants to be safe when they are home alone for short periods.

Social Ready™

Ages 8–14 • 60 minutes • In-person or virtual • 6-20 participants

An interactive workshop that teaches phone safety, digital boundaries, handling group chats, and recognizing red flags before kids head online.

Screen Ready™

Parents of young children • 60 minutes • In-person or virtual • 10–40 participants

A non-judgmental parent workshop covering what the research says about screen time and young children’s impulsivity, attention, and learning, what age-appropriate content looks like at different stages, and realistic routines, boundaries, and conversation starters that work in real households. Every parent takes home a one-page plan they can start using that night. Pairs with Social Ready™: run the student session and the parent session in the same week.

Steady and Ready™: Inclusive Safety and Readiness

Kids and teens, ages 8–12 • 60 minutes • In-person or virtual • 6–8 participants

A neurodivergent-affirming safety class designed to build essential emergency readiness skills in a low-pressure environment.

Middle School Ready™: Navigating Middle School

Grades 5–7 • 60 minutes • In-person or virtual • Up to 20 participants

A workshop that helps kids entering or starting middle school navigate the building, the rules, and the adults: asking for help and being heard, handling misunderstandings, and finding trusted adults.

Stay Ready™: Choking Rescue, CPR, and Seizure Response

Ages 11 and up • 75 minutes • In-person or virtual • 6-12 participants

A hands-on refresher for choking rescue, CPR, and seizure response.

Lunchroom Lifesaver™: Choking Response for School Staff and Volunteers

School staff and volunteers • 45 minutes • In-person • 6–12 participants • Lunchroom Ready Day: up to 6 back-to-back sessions • Evening or weekend parent session

A practical, hands-on class with no certification: recognizing choking, giving back blows and abdominal thrusts, responding if a child becomes unresponsive, calling 911, and the basics of hands-only CPR and using an AED. Taught at the school during the day, in the evening, or on a weekend.

Ready. Period.™

Girls ages 10–12 • 90 minutes • In-person or virtual • 6–18 participants

Girls build confidence around puberty: they design their own period kit while learning body literacy and self-advocacy in a supportive setting.

American Red Cross certification

After School Clubs

After school clubs for elementary schools. We come to your school and bring everything.

Each club meets one hour a week after dismissal. Kids practice real skills in small groups: role play, build and make things, and leave each session with something to use at home. Clubs run as a standalone offering, through an after-school enrichment provider, or as a PTA program, and more than one club can run at the same time. Every club is also offered in Spanish. A minimum of 6 participants is needed to run a club.

My First Babysitter's Club™

Ages 8–10 • 8-week series (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8-12 participants

Also offered as a standalone 2.5-hour workshop.

The first step toward babysitting. Members learn to be a Parent's Helper: a trusted extra set of hands while a parent is home, with safety lessons and real practice each week.

Little Medics

Ages 5–8 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

A first aid club for the youngest helpers: what an emergency is, how to get help fast, and care for scrapes, bumps, bug bites and nosebleeds.

Pet Sitter's Club

Ages 8–11 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

For kids who want to care for animals: what pets need, staying safe around unfamiliar animals, and running a pet sitting job from first visit to final payment.

Snack Squad

Ages 7–11 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

A no-stove kitchen club: kitchen safety, food allergies, reading labels, and building a balanced snack every week.

Kid Boss Club

Ages 9–11 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

Young entrepreneurs turn an idea into a real product: simple budgets, pricing, pitches, and a final pop-up market for families.

Middle School, Here I Come

Ages 10–11 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

A head start on the biggest school change yet: lockers, schedules, planners, keeping track of six teachers, and speaking up.

Big Sibling Club

Ages 5–9 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

For kids with a baby or little sibling: gentle, safe ways to help, what babies need, and handling big feelings about a new sibling.

My First Phone

Ages 9–11 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

For kids getting a first phone: passwords, privacy, group chats, screen time, and what to do the moment something online feels wrong.

Walk and Roll Ready

Ages 7–11 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

Getting around the neighborhood safely on foot, bike or scooter: road rules, helmets, hand signals, and planning a safe route.

Calm and Ready

Ages 5–11 • 8 weeks (1 hour/week) • In-person, at your school • 8–16 participants

A feelings toolbox for every kid: naming emotions, breathing and calm-down tools that work in the moment, at school and at home.
