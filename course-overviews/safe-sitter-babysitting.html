Safe Sitter® Babysitting

Students learn to keep children safe, handle an emergency without panicking, and run a babysitting business they can be proud of.

AT A GLANCE

AGESGrades 6 through 9
LENGTHFive hours, with snack breaks and a meal break
TAUGHT BYCertified teachers and registered nurses
CERTIFICATIONSafe Sitter, Inc. certificate

SAFETY AND EMERGENCIES

Recognizing an emergency and staying calm

CARING FOR CHILDREN

Ages and stages, infants through school age

Holding, feeding and diapering an infant

Interviewing with a family

Knowing when to say no to a job

A NOTE ON CERTIFICATION

After completion, students can tell future employers that they are a certified Safe Sitter®. Students learn choking rescue, first aid and introductory rescue skills. The course does not issue a CPR certification card. Please note that while participants gain essential, hands-on skills in infant and child CPR tailored specifically for babysitters, this course does not grant a standalone CPR certification. A formal, standalone CPR certification requires over three hours of instruction on its own.

Instead, our full-day Safe Sitter® curriculum provides comprehensive training across multiple vital areas (including child safety, emergency response, first aid, and behavioral management) alongside age-appropriate, targeted CPR instruction. This ensures students receive practical, real-world training without requiring a multi-day commitment.
