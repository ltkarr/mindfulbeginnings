How did you hear about us?
Based on what families select on the registration form
Partner referrals
Families who named one of your community partners on the registration form. Commission is the same flat rate as an originated booking, on that child’s revenue after processing fees, their materials and their share of instructor pay.
Net is what the family paid, less the PayPal or Venmo fee, less that child’s handbook and materials, less their share of the instructor fee and any job costs on that session. The instructor share is split across the families who have paid, so mark a roster up to date before you run a statement — with half the class still showing unpaid, the one child you are looking at carries more of the instructor than they should. Nothing is payable until the money has landed and the class has been taught, and commission never goes below zero.
In-kind donations (auction prizes)

Discount code usage
