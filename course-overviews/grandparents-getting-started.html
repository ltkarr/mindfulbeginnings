Safe Sitter® Grandparents: Getting Started

A NOTE ON CERTIFICATION

Participants practice choking rescue and CPR on infant and child manikins with hands-on coaching. The course does not issue a CPR certification card, because a standalone certification requires over three hours of instruction on its own.

Instead, these three hours cover child development, child care duties, behavior management, injury prevention, first aid and communication alongside targeted infant and child CPR instruction. The content follows guidelines from the American Academy of Pediatrics and the American Heart Association. Anyone who wants a card can add a Friends and Family® CPR class.
